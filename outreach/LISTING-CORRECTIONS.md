## Why together

Google, Bing and TripAdvisor all publish the same wrong opening hours, and two
of the three spell the address the same wrong way. Two independent facts wrong
identically across three platforms is one seeding source, not three mistakes.
Fixing one and leaving the others means the wrong version stays authoritative
on two surfaces — and Bing feeds Copilot and a share of ChatGPT, so it
propagates into AI answers about the business.
