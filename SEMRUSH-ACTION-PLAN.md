Four in five ranking keywords are informational and they are what brings people
in. But 35 transactional keywords carry nearly a third of all sessions — each is
worth roughly ten informational ones. The site is excellent at attracting
readers and structurally bad at handing them to a page that takes a booking.

Seven clusters defined, each as one MONEY page + one HUB + its informational
SPOKES. The audit then checks whether each spoke actually links up into both.

**Fixed this round:** /kainchi-dham had a Kumaon-loop CTA that went only to
WhatsApp — commercial intent with no crawlable link. It now links to
`/packages/kainchi-dham-nainital-mukteshwar-3n-4d` twice: once in the body where
the loop is already described, once as a button beside the WhatsApp CTA. That is
the single highest-value internal link on the site — 21.8% of all sessions
entered through that page and none of them were being offered the trip.

**Deliberately not automated.** The audit reports gaps; it does not inject a link
block into 55 pages. A loop-rendered link row is a footer and Google reads it as
chrome. Close these by hand where the sentence already wants to make the point —
the Kainchi fix above is the pattern.

The audit also caught a stale slug: `/char-dham-yatra-checklist` 301s to
`/blog/char-dham-yatra-packing-list`. It now names the redirect target instead of
just reporting a dead slug.

# SEMrush Round 4 — August 27, 2026
Competitor exports and the gap analysis. Closes the "next" item from Round 3.

All 13 tracked competitors pulled from Semrush Backlink Analytics. 12 produced
data (~90-100 referring domains each, 1,147 rows total);
**triptochardham.in returns "No matching results" in Semrush** and has no export,
so the register's note that its links are "mostly travel blogs" stays unverified.

**775 distinct referring domains. 64 link to 2+ competitors and not to us.
All 64 scored WEAK — zero pursue, zero maybe.**

The shared link graph of this niche is bookmark farms (`video-bookmark.com`,
`bookmark4you.com`, `socialbookmarkssite.com`), 2005-era directories
(`trafficdirectory.org`, `piratedirectory.org`, `alive-directory.com`) and search
engines indexing a page (`bing.com`, `yahoo.com`). `kumartravelsharidwar.com`
outranks us on the local operator term with roughly thirty referring domains
literally named `bookmark<something>.com`. The links are not why it ranks.

**79 of the 775 already link to us** — the shared farm, quantified from the other
side. A tenth of the niche's link graph arrives at every operator automatically.

So the 2-competitor threshold is the wrong lens here: the only sites promiscuous
enough to link to six Haridwar operators are the ones that link to everybody. The
useful tier is sites linking to exactly **one** competitor — a single editorial
choice beats six automated ones. 16 candidates were pulled from that tier and
live-audited.
